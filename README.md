#Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) Analysis

This analysis focuses on estimating the long-term value generated by customers using subscription and account data. Customer information was first combined with revenue and retention data to create a unified dataset for analysis. Customers were then segmented based on available attributes such as plan tier, acquisition source, industry, and geographic region to evaluate differences in customer behavior and value.

Average Order Value (AOV) was calculated by dividing the total revenue generated by a customer segment by the total number of transactions within that segment. Purchase Frequency was determined by measuring the average number of transactions made by customers over the analysis period.

Using these metrics, Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) was calculated to estimate the total revenue expected from a customer throughout their relationship with the business. Retention and subscription duration data were incorporated to better understand customer lifespan and its impact on long-term revenue generation.

The analysis included comparing CLTV across multiple customer segments to identify high-value and low-value customer groups. These insights help highlight which customer segments contribute the most revenue over time and provide a foundation for improving retention strategies, optimizing customer acquisition efforts, and supporting data-driven business decisions.
