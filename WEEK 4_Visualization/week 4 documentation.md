## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains the Week 4 deliverable of a customer subscription analytics project completed as part of my Data Analytics Internship at **Infotact Solutions**.

Over the previous three weeks, raw subscription data was cleaned, cohorts were built, and retention/CLTV metrics were calculated. Week 4 focuses on the final and most important stage of any analytics project — **turning those numbers into clear, decision-ready visuals**. This notebook takes the processed outputs from Weeks 1–3 and builds a complete visual story around customer retention, cohort performance, and customer lifetime value (CLTV), the kind of reporting a real business stakeholder would actually use to make decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Visualize cohort-wise retention behavior over time using a heatmap
- Track how retention decays month-over-month across the customer base
- Identify the strongest-performing acquisition cohorts
- Analyze Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) across different subscription plan tiers
- Compare retention rates across plan tiers and industries to spot at-risk segments

## 🔍 Analysis Walkthrough

### 1. Environment Setup & Data Loading
All Week 1–3 outputs are loaded into memory, and a clean, consistent visual theme (`whitegrid`) is applied across every chart in the notebook for a unified look.

### 2. Cohort Retention Heatmap
A heatmap of monthly retention percentages across all acquisition cohorts (Jan 2023 – Dec 2024), making it easy to spot which cohorts retained customers well and where drop-off was steepest.

📊 Output: `cohort_retention_heatmap_final.png`

### 3. Average Retention Decay Curve
A line chart tracking the average retention rate across all cohorts as months since acquisition increase, with a 50% retention benchmark line and an annotation flagging the steepest drop — a roughly **40% decline occurring right at Month 1**.

📊 Output: `retention_decay_curve.png`

### 4. Top 10 Cohorts by Retention
A ranked bar chart highlighting the ten acquisition cohorts with the strongest average retention, each labeled with its exact retention percentage.

📊 Output: `top_cohorts_bar.png`

### 5. Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) Analysis
A two-panel view combining:
- Average CLTV compared across **Enterprise**, **Pro**, and **Basic** plan tiers
- A scatter plot relating purchase frequency to CLTV, showing how usage patterns connect to long-term customer value

📊 Output: `cltv_analysis.png`

### 6. Retention by Plan Tier & Industry
A side-by-side comparison of retention rates segmented by plan tier and by industry, useful for identifying which customer segments are most loyal and which may need retention-focused intervention.

📊 Output: `retention_by_segment.png`

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Customer retention drops sharply within the first month of a subscription (~40% decline), signaling that **early-lifecycle engagement is the biggest lever** for improving long-term retention.
- CLTV increases meaningfully with plan tier, with **Enterprise customers driving the highest lifetime value**, followed by Pro and Basic.
- A small set of acquisition cohorts consistently outperform the rest, suggesting the acquisition channels or onboarding conditions behind them are worth replicating.
- Retention varies noticeably across industries and plan tiers, pointing to specific segments where churn-prevention efforts would have the greatest impact.
