# Cohort Retention Methodology

## Step 1: Customer Grouping

Customers are grouped into cohorts based on the month of their first subscription.

Example:

* January 2024 customers → January Cohort
* February 2024 customers → February Cohort

## Step 2: Retention Tracking

For each cohort, customer activity is tracked across subsequent months.

Retention is calculated by measuring the percentage of customers who remain active after their initial subscription month.

## Step 3: Cohort Matrix Creation

Rows represent acquisition cohorts.

Columns represent retention periods:

* Month 0
* Month 1
* Month 2
* Month 3

Each cell contains the percentage of customers retained.

## Step 4: Visualization

The retention matrix will be visualized using a heatmap to identify retention trends and churn patterns.

## Expected Insight

The cohort analysis will help identify the periods where customer drop-off is highest and highlight cohorts with strong long-term retention.
